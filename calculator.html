How this works

The calculator uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, one of the standard methods for estimating resting metabolic rate. It multiplies that figure by an activity factor to estimate what you use across a whole day.

It is an equation, not a measurement. Two people with identical height, weight, age and activity can have genuinely different needs. Treat the result as a starting point to adjust from, and pay attention to how you actually feel: energy, hunger, sleep and mood tell you more than any calculator.

A note on going low

This site will not generate a very low target. If a calculation lands below the level generally considered a sensible floor for adults, it stops there and suggests speaking to a healthcare professional instead. Very low intakes are something to do with proper support, not from a website.
